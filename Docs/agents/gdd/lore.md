# Lore Reference

Condensed from GDD appendix A. Facts only from universe wiki and internal KONTUR documents. Nothing invented.

## A.1 — Fungus O-41

- Grows inside **concrete** structures; separate strain for **wood** (Object 15, Troitskoye)
- After **sochlenenie** (stable colony forms) a space becomes an **Object** — distorted space with its own geometry and entities
- Spreads via: infected people touching walls with mucous membranes ("licking"); sign = **tap water turns black**
- Growth faster in darkness; residential building hallways are kept lit intentionally as countermeasure
- Field measurement device: **Baz M**

## A.2 — Stabilization and ASS cameras

- **Human presence stabilizes an Object** — anomalies and entities do not change while observed or when someone is nearby
- **ASS (Autonomous Surveillance System):** cameras with motion sensors + autonomous power; **can substitute for a human** in the stabilization role
- Without activity: records at reduced frame rate (battery/film conservation)
- Confirmed case (diggers, sealed building): stabilizing cameras held Object in one stable state; when recording/sensors failed — space began changing, entity emerged
- **Simulation collapse:** accompanied by sudden sound, darkening, mimics entering active phase

## A.3 — Spore Syndrome (human infection)

- Spores infect the brain; transmitted by breathing near infected individuals
- Stopped by gas mask filters — staff are required to wear them
- Mutation threshold: **100 spores/μL** (test "Lunch")
- **Goncharov Test:** place chalk pieces → infected compulsively eats it (diagnostic)
- **Sporinol** (component A-93): containment drug; used in both field protocols and the grandmother experiment

## A.5 — Protocols and KONTUR organization

- **K.O.N.T.U.R.** — Control of Objects of Unknown Type and Conditional Reality; founded 1976; hotline **0-41**
- **MOS (Junior operational staff):** assess incident, mark Object boundary, sanitary cordon, evacuation; entry/contact prohibited without necessity → **Protocol #2**
- **Protocol #2** (on entity contact): take cover (one entry point), **do not sleep / do not close eyes** (triggers Object regeneration), imitate environment behavior, do not eat or drink, await evacuation, signal (3 short — pause — 1 long)
- **Cover legend:** "gas leak"; vehicles disguised as emergency gas service; witnesses hospitalized "with CO poisoning"
- **Emergency protocol** (loss of team): spore capsule in watch body → triggers acute syndrome, mobilizes person for ~4 days
- **Church of Sprouting** — cult deliberately infecting people; source of the grandmother's infection (cf. Irkutsk and Kaliningrad incidents)
