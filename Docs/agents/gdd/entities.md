# GDD extract: Spore syndrome, entities, protocols

Threat content beyond Babooshka — mostly future/optional. Every entity has a
**diegetic trigger rule**; the GDD explicitly avoids arbitrary rituals.

## Spore syndrome (infected people — resident events material)
- Spores attack the brain; spread by the breath of the infected; stopped by
  gas-mask filters (employees wear them).
- Signs: craving to gnaw chalk/concrete, apathy/forgetfulness, attraction to
  smooth concrete, "mooing" vocalization that releases spores, rapid weight
  loss, unnatural smile, thinning limbs.
- States like entities: passive/active. **"Distress"**: an infected person
  realizes they are being watched → anxiety and directed aggression at the
  witness.
- **Goncharov test**: lay out chalk pieces — an infected person can't resist
  eating them (diagnostic).
- Mutation threshold: 100 spores/µl. **Sporinol** (A-93) = containment drug
  (same one used on Babooshka).

## Org & protocol flavor (UI copy, events)
- **K.O.N.T.U.R.** — Control of Objects of Unknown Type and Conditional
  Reality (est. 1976). Emergency phone **0-41**. Cover story: "gas leak";
  witnesses hospitalized "with carbon-monoxide poisoning".
- **Protocol #2** (entity contact): hide in a spot with one entry point, don't
  sleep or close your eyes, mimic the environment, eat/drink nothing, await
  evacuation, signal: 3 short — pause — 1 long.
- Emergency protocol (group lost): spore capsule in a watch casing → acute
  syndrome, ~4 days of mobilization.
- **Church of Sprouting** — cult that infects people deliberately (source of
  Babooshka's infection).
