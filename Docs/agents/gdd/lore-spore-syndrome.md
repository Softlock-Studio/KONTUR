# Lore — Spore Syndrome (Resident Infection)

## Transmission
- Spores affect the brain; transmitted by breathing near an infected person.
- Stopped by gas mask filters — employees are required to wear them.

## Symptoms
- Urge to gnaw chalk / concrete (exophthalmos).
- Apathy, forgetfulness.
- Strange craving for smooth concrete.
- "Mooing" / vocalization with spore release.
- Sharp weight loss.
- Unnatural smile.
- Limb thinning.

## Behavioral states (analogous to entity states)
- **Passive** — infected person does not know they are being watched.
- **Active** — infected person noticed observation.
- **"Anxiety"** — infected realizes they are being watched → alarm and directed
  aggression toward the witness.

## Diagnostics
- **Goncharov test:** place chalk pieces — infected person is irresistibly drawn
  to eat it.
- **Mutation threshold:** changes begin at **100 spores/µl** (test "Lunch").

## Treatment
- **Sporinol** — control drug based on component **A-93**.
  Referenced in: homeless woman's den, grandmother experiment.
