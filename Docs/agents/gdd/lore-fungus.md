# Lore — Fungus O-41

## Biology
- Grows inside **concrete** structures.
- Wood variant also exists — Object 15 (Troitsk).
- After **sochlenenie** (stable colony formation), the location becomes an **Object** —
  distorted space with its own geometry and entities.

## Spread
- Via infected humans: touching walls with mucous membranes, "licking."
- Sign of infection: **water in tap turns black.**
- **Grows faster in darkness.** Residential building hallways kept lit intentionally.

## Combat reagents (ascending strength)

| Reagent | Notes |
| ------- | ----- |
| **Vinegar 9%** | Only household reagent; acidifies concrete pores; cheap |
| **Carpets on walls** (3–6 pcs) | "Natural compress"; reduces initial anomalies; prevents Vestnik appearance; spray with vinegar several times a week |
| **KONTUR treatment** (with iodine-resorcinol-A) | Slows growth by **89%** |
| **Iodine-resorcinol-A** | Reduces infection to minimum for extended time; used on critical infrastructure (M1, government buildings, nuclear plants); sharp "metro smell"; causes aversion in infected (repels) |
