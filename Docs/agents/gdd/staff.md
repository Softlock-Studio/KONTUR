# Staff Management

## Interaction model
- Player assigns tasks to staff via UI; does not control staff directly
- Interaction: click staff → click room → context menu with available actions
- Player interacts with only 1 staff member at a time

## Task types

| Task | Effect | Duration |
|------|--------|----------|
| Treat zone | Apply vinegar or iodine-resorcinol → reduces infection | 30–60 game minutes |
| Replace bulb | Adds lighting; better camera visibility; infection grows slower | TBD |
| Repair camera | Activates a broken camera | TBD |
| Actions with grandmother | Summon her, leave food | TBD |
| Investigate room | Recon; detect new hotspots/threats | TBD |
| Work with residents | Respond to events/requests | TBD |
