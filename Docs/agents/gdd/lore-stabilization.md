# Lore — Object Stabilization

**This is the mechanical foundation of the camera system.**

## Core rule
**Human presence stabilizes an Object** — anomalies and entities do not change
while being observed or while someone is nearby.

## ACS cameras as stabilizers
- **ACS (Autonomous Surveillance System)** — cameras with motion sensors and
  autonomous power.
- Can **perform the stabilization function instead of a human**.
- Without activity, ACS records at reduced frame rate (battery/film conservation).

## Evidence (digger incident, sealed building)
"Stabilizing cameras on the perimeter fixed the Object in one stable state."
When recording/sensors failed → space began changing, entity emerged.

## Simulation destruction
- Accompanies: sharp sound, darkening, mimics enter active phase.

## Implication for gameplay
Keeping cameras active and employees present in zones stabilizes those zones.
Neglected zones (no cameras, no employees) can change state.
