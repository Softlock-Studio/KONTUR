# Lore — Protocols and Organization

## K.O.N.T.U.R.
- Full name: Контроль Объектов Неизвестного Типа и Условной Реальности.
- Founded: **1976**. Emergency phone: **0-41**.

## MOS (Junior Operational Staff)
- Responsibilities: incident assessment, establishing Object borders, sanitary cordon,
  civilian evacuation.
- Entry and contact prohibited without necessity → **Protocol #2**.

## Protocol #2 (on entity contact)
1. Shelter — one entry point only.
2. Do not sleep / do not close eyes (else Object regenerates).
3. Imitate the behavior of the environment.
4. Eat and drink nothing.
5. Await evacuation.
6. Signal: **3 short — pause — 1 long.**
