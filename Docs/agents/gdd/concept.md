# Concept

The player controls the K.O.N.T.U.R. task force, which is containing a fungal
infection (O-41) in a residential apartment building.

- Player stays in the **observation room** — does not participate directly in operations.
- Via the camera system and management interface, the player coordinates employees,
  assigns tasks, and reacts to threats.
- In parallel: infection spreads, **random events with residents** occur, and an infected
  elderly woman (experiment participant) roams the building as the primary threat.
- Goal each night: keep infection under control, keep the team alive.
