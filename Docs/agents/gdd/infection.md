# Infection System

## Zones
- Building divided into zones (rooms, corridor)
- Each zone has one infection parameter: 0–100%; no sub-scales
- Building indicator = average across all zones; displayed on screen

## Spread
- Infection spreads gradually through the building during the night
- Spread is faster in darkness
- After each night, overall infection level increases

## Measurement
- Full house scan (button): removes fog of war across all zones simultaneously
- Costs generator fuel
- Effect persists for a duration, then data begins to age again (fog returns)
