# Camera System

## Functions
- Switch between cameras placed throughout the building

## What cameras enable (all visual, shown in camera feed)
- Track staff positions
- Observe the grandmother's position
- Find new tasks (dirt to clean, broken cameras, etc.)
- Detect new threats (e.g., visual markers like the Vestnik — indicator of an unnoticed hotspot in frame)

## ASS (Autonomous Surveillance System)
- Cameras with motion sensors and autonomous power
- Can perform stabilization function in place of a human (lore: anomalies and entities don't change while observed)
- When inactive: records at reduced frame rate (battery/film conservation)
- Powered by generator fuel
