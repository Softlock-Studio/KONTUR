# Grandmother (Main Threat)

## Overview
- Infected elderly woman, experiment participant — current main threat
- Freely roams the building most of the time
- At certain moments of the night, moves purposefully toward staff
- Upon encounter: kills the staff member
- Behavior must be rule-predictable while creating tactical uncertainty
- Kill probability (%): derived from overall building infection level (TBD formula)
